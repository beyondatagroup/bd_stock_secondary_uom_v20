Overview

Product Secondary Unit of Measure

The Product Secondary Unit of Measure module enhances Odoo's product and inventory management by allowing users to define and manage a secondary Unit of Measure (UoM) for products. Users can configure a secondary UoM along with its conversion ratio and use it across key business operations such as Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, and Inventory Transfers.

This helps businesses manage products that are commonly measured or transacted using two different units, while maintaining accurate quantity calculations between the primary and secondary UoMs.

SCREENSHOTS

Configuration

1. User Access Rights

  • Before using the Secondary Unit of Measure functionality in Purchase, assign the appropriate access groups to the users.

  • Navigate to Settings → Users & Companies → Users

  • Open the required user and assign the relevant access group.

Secondary Unit on Product

  • Assign the Secondary Unit on Product group to users who need to configure Secondary Unit of Measure on products.

  • This allows users to access the Secondary Unit configuration available on the Product form.

2. Enable Units of Measure

  • After configuring user access, enable Odoo's standard Units of Measure functionality.

  • Navigate to Inventory → Configuration → Settings

  • Enable Units of Measure & Packagings and save the configuration.

3. Configure Secondary Unit on Product

  • Go to Inventory → Products → Products

  • Open the required product.

  • On the Product form, enable Use Secondary Unit and select the required Secondary Unit of Measure.
